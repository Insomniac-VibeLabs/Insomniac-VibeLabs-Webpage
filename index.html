Who we are

People who can't sleep. The lights go off, the brain doesn't, and an idea shows up instead. So the idea gets vibe coded until our eyelids finally give up.

Later, in daylight or close to it, we pick it back up and build on it as a hobby. Some of it grows. Some of it stays a weird little experiment. Both are fine.

What's been built so far

The first projects happen to lean toward security and AI. That's where the 3 a.m. thoughts went, not a declared focus.

  • two-key-concept

    The trimmed-down version of the idea: two independent keys have to turn before an AI agent can act. A policy VM, a panel of judges, single-use tokens, and a ledger, with the extras left out.

  • two-key

    Where it started. The same concept with the whole kitchen sink attached, which is why the concept version exists.

Plans and roadmaps

There aren't any. No strategy, no milestones, no schedule. Things get worked on when there's energy and curiosity, usually at an hour most people would call unreasonable.

Everything is public on GitHub if you want to poke around.
